THE ABOUT

Built from music, scaled into a platform.

Michael Collins Ajereh, known professionally as Don Jazzy, is a singer, songwriter, producer and entrepreneur whose public story spans production, label building and brand building.

Role Producer / Executive

He first built his name in the Mo’ Hits era, where the focus was record-making, artist identity and sound. That chapter gave him the base that later became a much larger platform.

In 2012, he launched Mavin Records. Mavin now presents itself as an African leading entertainment company and publicly lists artists such as Ayra Starr, Ladipoe, Johnny Drille, Crayon, Magixx, Bayanni, Boy Spyce, DJ Big N, Lifesize Teddy and Rema, alongside producers and writers including Andre Vibez, JVXN, Mbryo, Milar, Prestige and Sevn. That is the point of the story: a label built as a system, not just a brand name.

Outside music, Jazzy’s Burger adds a consumer brand layer that extends the identity into food and lifestyle. Keep that chapter separate in the site so the structure stays clean, simple and professional.

THE MAVIN ERA

A label built as a system, not just a name.

After the Mo’ Hits chapter, Don Jazzy launched Mavin Records in 2012. The label became a larger structure for music, talent development, branding and long-term cultural presence.

Mavin’s official site presents the company as an African leading entertainment company. That framing matters because it shows the scale of the operation: not only releases, but artists, writers, producers, services and a wider creative ecosystem.

The public artist roster includes names such as Ayra Starr, Bayanni, Boy Spyce, CupidSZN, Johnny Drille, LADIPOE, Lovn, Magixx and Rema. The public services page also points to brand partnerships, music licensing, performance and content production.

This is why the section should feel like an institution page instead of a normal about block. The label should read as a machine that discovers, packages and sustains talent.
